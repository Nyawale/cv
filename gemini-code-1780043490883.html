About Me

I am a multifaceted development professional with a deep academic grounding in macroeconomics and public policy. Over the last decade, I have driven initiatives across East Africa focused on promoting digital civic spaces, building research capacity, and designing economic empowerment programmes.

My work combines structured quantitative insights with local community advocacy. As a dynamic communicator, I work to bridge the gap between rigorous technical findings and actionable development policies that empower citizens.
