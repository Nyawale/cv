About Me

I am a Tanzanian economist, researcher, and digital governance specialist with over 7 years of experience in public policy, taxation, financial inclusion, media freedom, and development research.

My work sits at the intersection of economic policy, digital finance, and behavioural insights. I am passionate about evidence-based policy that promotes financial inclusion and good governance in East Africa.
