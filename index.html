About
Research-driven public policy and digital governance work

Francis Stephen Nyonzo is an economist and researcher with seven years of experience in program coordination, policy analysis, economic development, and content leadership across NGOs and international development settings.

His work focuses on mobile money, taxation, informal markets, financial inclusion, journalism, civic education, and governance. He also writes and trains on statistical analysis, proposal development, research methods, and communication.

He is currently a Rotary Peace Fellow for 2026–27 and will study for a Postgraduate Diploma in Peace Building and Conflict Transformation at Makerere University.
