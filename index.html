Who I Am

Economist, Author & Researcher

I am a Tanzanian economist, author, researcher and journalist with a multifaceted background spanning development economics, digital journalism, programme management and civic advocacy. My experience in the newsroom began in 2016 as a news reporter at an English-language newspaper in Tanzania, and I have since contributed to numerous regional and international outlets.

My research focuses on digital economy, taxation, mobile money, regional trade integration, behavioural economics, and economics of crime — with a sustained lens on East Africa and the African continent. I believe development is an outcome of rigorous, evidence-based research translated into actionable policy.

As a Fulbright Hubert H. Humphrey Fellow at Michigan State University (2024–25), I deepened expertise in data science, behavioural economics and international trade policy. I am currently completing a Master of Arts in Digital Journalism at Aga Khan University, Nairobi.

Beyond academia, I am a Writing Fellow at African Liberty, a practitioner promoting digital democracy at JamiiAfrica, and an active peer reviewer for the African Tax Research Network (ATRN).
